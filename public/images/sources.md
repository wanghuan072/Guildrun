# Image sources

The production-facing image files in this folder are local derivatives used for editorial identification and commentary.

- Official Steam screenshots: `https://store.steampowered.com/api/appdetails?appids=3669200&cc=us&l=english`
  - Used for homepage, gameplay, progression, world, guide, and update scenes.
- Official Guildrun website: `https://www.playguildrun.com/`
  - Used for the official hero lineup and Rift backdrop.
- Guildrun Wiki game-data reference: `https://guildrun.wiki/`
  - Used for in-game extracted hero portraits and item/relic icons. These are labeled as game-data references, not developer press assets.

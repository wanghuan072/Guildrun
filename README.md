# Guildrun Guide

[Guildrun.net](https://guildrun.net/) is an independent English-language guide, wiki, and strategy reference for **Guildrun**, the single-player PvE roguelike autobattler developed by Leyline.

Use the site to learn the run loop, compare Heroes, understand classes and ranks, search items and relics, inspect enemies and stages, review event choices, and turn each defeat into a better formation decision.

> This is an unofficial fan resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Leyline.

## What Is Guildrun?

Guildrun combines roguelike progression with autobattler combat. During a run, you recruit Heroes, equip items, choose relics, improve ranks, select specializations, and arrange your formation before combat begins. Battles resolve automatically, so success depends on preparation: identifying the next threat, assigning clear jobs to each Hero, controlling first contact, and spending resources on the weakness that ended the previous fight.

The game rewards flexible planning. A strong run is not simply a collection of high-stat units. It is a connected team with an anchor, reliable damage, support or control, useful class interactions, and a formation that allows those pieces to perform their jobs.

## Core Features

- **Roguelike runs:** Build a new team through changing shops, rewards, stages, events, and route decisions.
- **Automatic combat:** Position the formation and prepare the build, then study the result instead of issuing commands during the fight.
- **Distinct Heroes:** Compare attack range, classes, abilities, base statistics, rank gains, specializations, and practical team roles.
- **Rank progression:** Develop Heroes from their base Rank C kit through specialization and modifier choices at later ranks.
- **Items and relics:** Combine direct stat bonuses, conditional effects, quests, triggers, and status interactions.
- **Formation strategy:** Decide who receives the first attack, which target your damage dealer can reach, and when support abilities will resolve.
- **Connected world systems:** Plan around stages, enemy formations, events, crossroads, fight modes, and permanent stat changes.
- **Multiple strategic goals:** Prepare for regular progression, difficult encounters, Red Rift pressure, and Endless scaling.

## Beginner Tips

- Give every active Hero a clear job: absorbing pressure, dealing damage, supporting allies, controlling enemies, or solving a specific matchup.
- Inspect enemy range and formation before moving your own units. First contact often determines whether the rest of the build can function.
- Do not buy an upgrade only because it is individually strong. Buy it because it fixes a visible problem or improves a reliable engine.
- Keep positioning flexible. A formation that wins one encounter may expose the carry or delay an important cast in the next.
- Treat Rank B specialization as a direction, not a guarantee that every later reward will support the same plan.
- Watch the first meaningful failure in each fight: an early death, a missed target, an interrupted cast, wasted movement, or insufficient damage.
- Recheck balance-sensitive values after an update. Strategy principles may remain useful even when exact numbers change.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Guildrun an action game?

No. Guildrun is an autobattler. You make recruitment, equipment, progression, and formation decisions before combat, then the battle resolves automatically.

### Is Guildrun.net the official Guildrun website?

No. [Guildrun.net](https://guildrun.net/) is an independent fan-made guide and reference site. The [official Guildrun website](https://www.playguildrun.com/) and Steam pages are the authoritative sources for announcements and store information.

### Where can I play Guildrun?

The [Guildrun Demo is available on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4425970/Guildrun_Demo/). You can also visit the [full-game Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3669200/Guildrun/) for store information and release status.

### What information does this guide cover?

The site covers the gameplay loop, Heroes, classes, guilds, stats, Mastery, items, relics, enemies, status effects, Rank Modifiers, stages, events, crossroads, fight modes, guides, patches, and release information.

### Which game version does the database use?

Each version-sensitive page displays its applicable Demo version or update date. Exact balance values can change, so consult the [Updates](https://guildrun.net/updates/) section whenever a newer patch is released.

### Where should a new player begin?

Start with the [Guildrun Beginner Guide](https://guildrun.net/guides/guildrun-beginner-guide/), then use the [Gameplay Handbook](https://guildrun.net/gameplay/) to diagnose specific problems with roles, enemies, positioning, Heroes, or economy decisions.

### Can I search the entire site?
